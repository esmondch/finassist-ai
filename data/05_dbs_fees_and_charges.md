## 1. Important rule before answering a fee question

DBS fees can depend on the card product, transaction type, currency, card network, date and applicable terms.

Before stating a fee, FinAssist AI must establish that the retrieved information supports all relevant details:

- The relevant card category or product.
- The type of transaction or fee.
- The applicable rate or fixed amount.
- Any minimum charge, additional charge or qualifying condition.
- The source page and its last-checked date.

If any of these are unclear, do not guess. Explain that the exact fee cannot be confirmed from the available knowledge base and direct the customer to the current official DBS rates-and-fees page.

## 2. Selected DBS credit-card charges

The linked DBS rates-and-fees page displays the following selected credit-card charges:

### Late payment charge

- **S$100 per account** if the minimum payment is not received by the card account's payment due date.

### Cash advance fee

- **8% of the amount withdrawn per transaction**, subject to a **minimum charge of S$15**.

### Cash advance finance charge

- The page displays a prevailing interest rate of **28.5% per annum** on the amount withdrawn, charged daily from the withdrawal date until full payment is received.
- The page also states a minimum finance charge of **S$2.50** and notes that compounding may apply if charges are not repaid in full.

### Finance charges on card transactions

- The page displays a prevailing rate of **27.8% per annum** where the full outstanding balance is not paid by the due date.
- The page describes daily calculation and explains that the charge applies to outstanding transactions until full payment is received.

### Minimum monthly repayment

- The page states **3% of the statement balance or S$50, whichever is greater**, plus any overdue amount and/or amount exceeding the credit limit.

**Important:** These are selected figures displayed on the source page at the date checked. They may change and may be subject to card-specific terms. Do not assume every DBS/POSB card or customer has identical terms.

## 3. Selected foreign-currency and overseas transaction information

### Foreign-currency retail transactions

The DBS page explains that foreign-currency transactions are converted to Singapore dollars using exchange rates determined by the relevant card association (such as Visa, Mastercard or American Express). The rate used may differ from the rate on the transaction date.

The page also describes charges imposed by card associations for foreign-currency transactions. The applicable treatment depends on the card/network and transaction.

Do not confuse the exchange rate used for currency conversion with a separate administrative fee or conversion factor.

### Singapore-dollar transactions processed outside Singapore — credit cards

The DBS page states that Singapore-dollar transactions processed outside Singapore, including transactions on overseas-based websites and mobile applications, are subject to an administrative fee of **1% by Visa or Mastercard**.

### Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) — Mastercard/Visa credit cards

The page displays a **1.00% administrative fee charged by the respective card associations** for DCC transactions. The page says the fee applies to DCC transactions, including purchases, refunds and reversals, and is applied to the converted SGD amount at processing.

### DBS debit cards — Singapore-dollar transactions processed outside Singapore

The DBS page states that transactions processed outside Singapore but charged in Singapore dollars are subject to an administrative fee of **2.8%** on the total transaction amount.

The page explains that this 2.8% fee includes the applicable network component:

- **1%** for Visa/Mastercard.
- **0.6%** for UnionPay.

**Important fee interpretation:**

- The 1% Visa/Mastercard or 0.6% UnionPay component is included in the stated 2.8% administrative fee for this transaction category.
- Do not add the 1% or 0.6% component on top of the 2.8%.
- Do not describe the network component as an additional fee charged separately by the merchant's bank unless an official source explicitly supports that claim.

### Overseas ATM withdrawals using debit cards

The DBS page describes card-association charges and conversion factors for overseas ATM withdrawals.

It states:

- A **1% conversion factor** for Visa/Mastercard-converted withdrawals.
- A **0.6% conversion factor** for foreign-currency transactions converted by UnionPay.
- An additional DBS administrative fee also applies.

**Important distinction:**

- These overseas ATM withdrawal charges are a different transaction category from Singapore-dollar transactions processed outside Singapore.
- The 2.8% administrative fee described in the previous subsection should not automatically be applied to overseas ATM withdrawals.
- The available information does not specify the exact additional DBS administrative fee for overseas ATM withdrawals.
- Do not calculate or claim a single all-in overseas ATM withdrawal fee from the conversion factors alone.
- Do not assume that the network conversion factor and the additional DBS administrative fee are interchangeable.

The final amount can depend on the network, currency conversion and applicable bank/card charges.

## 5. Rules for FinAssist AI

1. Never treat a fee from one card category as applying to every DBS/POSB card.
2. Distinguish debit-card charges from credit-card charges.
3. Distinguish a foreign-currency transaction from a Singapore-dollar transaction processed overseas.
4. Distinguish overseas ATM withdrawals from Singapore-dollar transactions processed overseas.
5. Do not confuse a card-network conversion factor with the complete fee charged to the customer.
6. Do not add a network component again when the source states that it is already included in an administrative fee.
7. Do not calculate a final charge unless the necessary rate, amount and conditions are all supported.
8. Do not present these figures as guaranteed current rates. Direct customers to the live DBS rates-and-fees page for confirmation.
9. If the question names a specific card and the retrieved passage does not identify that card, say that the available information is insufficient.
10. If an additional fee is mentioned but its amount is not available in the retrieved information, state that the amount is unspecified rather than estimating it.
