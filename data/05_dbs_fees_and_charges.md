## 1. Important rules before answering a fee question

DBS fees can depend on the card product, transaction type, currency, card network, date and applicable terms.

Before stating a fee, FinAssist AI must establish that the retrieved information supports all relevant details:

- The relevant card category or product.
- The type of transaction or fee.
- The applicable rate or fixed amount.
- Any minimum charge, additional charge or qualifying condition.
- Whether a network component is included in or charged separately from the administrative fee.
- The source page and its last-checked date.

If any of these details are unclear, do not guess. Explain that the exact fee cannot be confirmed from the available knowledge base and direct the customer to the current official DBS rates-and-fees page.

**Important:** A fee applying to one transaction category must not automatically be applied to another category, even if both involve overseas transactions.

## 2. Selected DBS credit-card charges

The linked DBS rates-and-fees page displays the following selected credit-card charges.

### Late payment charge

- **S$100 per account** if the minimum payment is not received by the card account's payment due date.

### Cash advance fee

- **8% of the amount withdrawn per transaction**, subject to a **minimum charge of S$15**.

### Cash advance finance charge

- The page displays a prevailing interest rate of **28.5% per annum** on the amount withdrawn, charged daily from the withdrawal date until full payment is received.
- The page also states a minimum finance charge of **S$2.50** and notes that compounding may apply if charges are not repaid in full.

### Finance charges on card transactions

- The page displays a prevailing rate of **27.8% per annum** where the full outstanding balance is not paid by the due date.
- The page describes daily calculation and explains that the charge applies to outstanding transactions until full payment is received.

### Minimum monthly repayment

- The page states **3% of the statement balance or S$50, whichever is greater**, plus any overdue amount and/or amount exceeding the credit limit.

### Important conditions

These are selected figures displayed on the source page at the date checked. They may change and may be subject to card-specific terms.

- Do not assume every DBS/POSB card or customer has identical terms.
- Do not treat these figures as guaranteed current rates.
- Do not calculate a customer's actual finance charge without the necessary balance, dates, applicable rate and repayment information.
- If the question names a specific card and the retrieved passage does not identify that card, explain that the available information is insufficient to confirm the card-specific fee.

## 3. Foreign-currency retail transactions

The DBS page explains that foreign-currency transactions are converted to Singapore dollars using exchange rates determined by the relevant card association, such as Visa, Mastercard or American Express.

The rate used may differ from the rate on the transaction date.

The page also describes charges imposed by card associations for foreign-currency transactions. The applicable treatment depends on the card network and transaction.

### Important distinctions

- The exchange rate used for currency conversion is not necessarily the same thing as a separate administrative fee.
- Do not assume every card network or card product has identical charges.
- Do not automatically apply the 2.8% DBS debit-card fee for overseas SGD transactions to foreign-currency retail purchases.
- Do not calculate a total foreign-currency fee unless the applicable rate and conditions are supported by the retrieved information.

## 4. Singapore-dollar transactions processed outside Singapore

A Singapore-dollar transaction processed outside Singapore is a different fee category from a foreign-currency transaction.

### Credit cards — Visa and Mastercard

The DBS page states that Singapore-dollar transactions processed outside Singapore, including transactions on overseas-based websites and mobile applications, are subject to an administrative fee of:

- **1% by Visa or Mastercard.**

Do not confuse this credit-card fee with the DBS debit-card fee described below.

### Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) — Mastercard/Visa credit cards

The page displays a **1.00% administrative fee charged by the respective card associations** for DCC transactions.

The page says the fee applies to DCC transactions, including purchases, refunds and reversals, and is applied to the converted SGD amount at processing.

Do not assume that this DCC fee applies to every foreign-currency transaction. DCC is a separate transaction category.

### DBS debit cards — Singapore-dollar transactions processed outside Singapore

For a DBS debit-card transaction processed outside Singapore but charged in Singapore dollars:

- The administrative fee is **2.8% of the total transaction amount**.
- The 2.8% fee includes the applicable card-network component.
- The Visa/Mastercard component is **1%**.
- The UnionPay component is **0.6%**.

**Critical no-double-counting rule:**

The applicable card-network component is already included in the 2.8% administrative fee. Do not add it again.

For example, for a Visa/Mastercard transaction:

- Correct applicable fee: **2.8%**.
- Incorrect calculation: **2.8% + 1% = 3.8%**.

The applicable fee remains 2.8%, not 3.8%.

This 2.8% fee applies to the specified category of Singapore-dollar transactions processed outside Singapore using DBS debit cards. It must not be generalised to:

- Foreign-currency retail purchases.
- Overseas ATM withdrawals.
- Credit-card transactions processed outside Singapore.

## 5. Overseas ATM withdrawals using debit cards

The DBS page describes card-association charges and conversion factors for overseas ATM withdrawals.

It states:

- **1% conversion factor** for Visa/Mastercard-converted withdrawals.
- **0.6% conversion factor** for foreign-currency transactions converted by UnionPay.
- An additional DBS administrative fee also applies.

### Important distinctions

- Overseas ATM withdrawal charges are a different transaction category from Singapore-dollar transactions processed outside Singapore.
- The 2.8% administrative fee described in Section 4 must not automatically be applied to overseas ATM withdrawals.
- The available information does not specify the exact additional DBS administrative fee for overseas ATM withdrawals.
- Do not calculate or claim a single all-in overseas ATM withdrawal fee from the conversion factors alone.
- Do not assume that the network conversion factor and the additional DBS administrative fee are interchangeable.

**Example question: What is the total fee for an overseas ATM withdrawal?**

The available information identifies the network conversion factor and states that an additional DBS administrative fee applies. However, the exact DBS administrative fee is not specified in this summary.

Therefore, FinAssist AI must state that the complete fee cannot be calculated from the available information rather than estimating a total.

## 7. Common questions and answer-generation guidance

### Question: Is the 2.8% fee applicable to overseas ATM withdrawals?

No. The 2.8% administrative fee described in this document applies to Singapore-dollar transactions processed outside Singapore using DBS debit cards. It should not automatically be applied to overseas ATM withdrawals.

### Question: Is the 1% Visa/Mastercard component an additional charge on top of the 2.8% fee?

No. For the specified DBS debit-card category of Singapore-dollar transactions processed outside Singapore, the applicable network component is already included in the 2.8% administrative fee.

### Question: Does the 2.8% fee apply to every overseas debit-card transaction?

No. It applies to the specified category of Singapore-dollar transactions processed outside Singapore. Do not generalise it to foreign-currency retail purchases or overseas ATM withdrawals.

### Question: What is the difference between an overseas SGD credit-card transaction and an overseas SGD debit-card transaction?

The selected information states:

- Visa/Mastercard credit cards: 1% administrative fee.
- DBS debit cards: 2.8% administrative fee, inclusive of the applicable network component.

These are separate card categories. Do not substitute one rate for the other.

### Question: Can FinAssist AI calculate the exact total fee for an overseas ATM withdrawal?

Not from this summary alone. The available information identifies a network conversion factor and an additional DBS administrative fee, but does not specify the exact additional fee. Do not estimate the total.

### Retrieval and answer-generation procedure

When answering a question about card fees:

1. Identify whether the customer is asking about a foreign-currency retail purchase, overseas SGD transaction, DCC transaction, cash advance or overseas ATM withdrawal.
2. Identify whether the question concerns a debit card or credit card.
3. Retrieve the corresponding fee and its conditions.
4. Check whether the card-network component is included in or separate from the administrative fee.
5. State any unspecified charges explicitly.
6. Only calculate a total when all applicable rates, amounts and conditions are supported.
7. If the available information is insufficient, explain what is missing instead of estimating.
