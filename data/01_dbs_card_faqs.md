# DBS Singapore — Card FAQs and Everyday Card Support

**Institution:** DBS Bank Singapore / POSB  
**Primary source:** [Bank Cards - FAQs](https://www.dbs.com.sg/personal/cards/cards-faqs.page)  
**Related source:** [DBS Cards Application Checklist](https://www.dbs.com.sg/personal/cards/cards-application-checklist.page)  
**Last checked:** 3 November 2026  
**Coverage:** Selected public information about personal DBS/POSB debit and credit cards.  
**Document type:** AI-assisted summary checked against the linked DBS webpages; not an official DBS publication.

## 1. Debit-card eligibility

The DBS card FAQ states that an applicant must be at least 16 years old and hold one of the following accounts:

- POSB Savings Account
- DBS Savings Plus Account
- DBS Autosave Account
- DBS Current Account

The FAQ states that foreigners should apply for a debit card at a DBS branch and present a passport and an Employment Pass valid for at least six months.

**Do not generalise:** These criteria are taken from the linked FAQ and may not cover every account, card type or exceptional application route. Refer customers to DBS for confirmation of their individual eligibility.

## 2. Credit-card eligibility

The DBS FAQ gives the following general criteria:

- Singapore citizens and permanent residents: at least 21 years old.
- Singapore citizens and permanent residents under 55: annual income of at least S$30,000.
- Singapore citizens and permanent residents aged 56 and above: annual income of at least S$15,000.
- Foreign applicants with a valid Employment Pass: annual income of at least S$45,000, unless otherwise stated.
- DBS Vantage Card: the FAQ states an annual income requirement of S$120,000.

The separate DBS Cards Application Checklist currently describes the Singapore citizen/PR age and income bands as 21 years old or above, S$30,000 for those aged 55 or below, and S$15,000 for those aged 56 to 75. It lists S$45,000 for foreigners.

**Important:** Eligibility can differ by card and applicant category. These figures are not an approval guarantee. Do not infer eligibility for a specific premium or co-branded card from the general criteria. Direct customers to the relevant card's application page and current checklist.

## 3. Card application processing time

The DBS FAQ asks applicants to allow **15 working days** for processing. It warns that applications missing required documents or containing incomplete information may take longer.

This is an application-processing estimate, not a promise that every application will be approved within 15 working days.

The FAQ says customers can check application status through Internet Banking under **Apply > More Application Services > View Application Status**, or use DBS's available digital support channels. The exact menu labels may change; customers should follow the current digibank interface.

## 4. Forgotten card PIN

The DBS FAQ lists these PIN-reset routes:

- DBS/POSB Internet Banking: **Cards > Reset Card PIN**
- DBS/POSB digibank app: **Card Services > Reset Card PIN**

Menu labels may change as the app is updated. Customers should use the official DBS/POSB app or website.

**Security:** FinAssist AI must never ask the customer to provide their PIN, password, OTP or Digital Token approval. A customer should enter credentials only into the official DBS authentication flow.

## 5. Card payment and statement questions

The DBS FAQ describes different payment-crediting times depending on the channel. For example, payments made through DBS Internet Banking/digibank are described as immediate for DBS/POSB account holders, while other channels may take longer.

The FAQ also explains that credit-card interest may apply when the full outstanding balance is not paid by the due date. Exact rates and charges should be checked against the current card rates-and-fees page and the customer's card terms.

Do not apply one payment timing or interest rate to every payment method or card product.

## 6. Cancelling, replacing and changing cards

For a lost or stolen debit or credit card, DBS instructs customers to report it immediately. The card can be blocked through DBS digital services or by contacting the dedicated fraud/lost-card hotline. See `04_dbs_lost_stolen_cards.md`.

For a damaged card, the FAQ directs customers to DBS digital services or the relevant customer service channel for a replacement.

Changing to a different card may require a new application because different cards have different terms. Do not promise that a card can be converted automatically.

## 7. Source limitations and answer rules

- This document covers selected FAQs, not every DBS card product or card agreement.
- Product benefits, eligibility, promotions, fees and conditions may differ.
- Do not promise application approval.
- Do not treat the 15-working-day processing estimate as a guaranteed completion date.
- Do not provide a fee or interest rate unless the relevant product, charge type, amount/rate and conditions are supported by a current retrieved source.
- If the customer's exact card or situation is unclear, ask for the non-sensitive card product name or direct them to DBS. Never request the full card number, PIN, password or OTP.
