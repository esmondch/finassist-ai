## 1. Act immediately

DBS instructs customers whose ATM, debit or credit card is lost or stolen to block the card immediately using its online services and request a replacement.

DBS lists a 24-hour Fraud, Scam and Lost Cards Reporting hotline:

- Singapore: **1800 339 6963**
- Overseas: **+65 6339 6963**

If the customer suspects fraud or unauthorised use, direct them to the official DBS hotline promptly rather than relying on this chatbot.

## 2. Block a lost card using digibank mobile

The DBS lost-card guide describes the following route:

1. Log in to the official digibank mobile app using the bank's authentication process.
2. Tap **More**.
3. Under **Manage Cards & Loans**, select **Replace/Block Card**.
4. Select **My Card is Lost**.
5. Tap **Proceed**.
6. Select the card to block.
7. Review the request and tap **Block Now**.
8. DBS states that the card will be blocked immediately. The customer may then proceed to request a replacement card.

The app interface may change. Customers should follow the latest instructions shown inside the official DBS app.

## 3. Block a lost card using digibank Online

The DBS guide describes this route:

1. Log in to digibank Online using the official DBS website.
2. Complete the authentication process.
3. Under **Home**, select **Cards** and choose the relevant card.
4. Under card details, select **Manage** and choose **Replace lost or stolen card**.
5. Select the type of card and follow the guided instructions in digibot to block and replace the card.

Customers should navigate to the official DBS website themselves. Do not direct them to login links supplied in unsolicited messages.

## 4. Requesting a replacement card

DBS states that customers may proceed to request a replacement after blocking a lost or stolen card.

The lost-card guide describes the blocking process and allows the customer to proceed with a replacement request. The separate DBS card-replacement guide provides additional instructions for replacement requests.

The exact steps and delivery arrangements may depend on the card type and replacement process.

**Important clarification:**

- Blocking a card and requesting a replacement are related but distinct actions.
- Do not claim that blocking a card automatically completes the replacement request.
- The available information does not establish one universal delivery method or delivery address for all replacement cards.
- Do not promise that a replacement card will be dispatched to the customer's registered address unless the relevant official source confirms this for that card and process.
- Do not quote a delivery timeline unless the retrieved official information supports it for the relevant card and replacement process.
- Direct customers to the current instructions in digibank or the official DBS replacement guide.

## 5. Lost or stolen card liability

The DBS lost-card page states that liability for unauthorised charges is limited to **S$100 only if specified conditions are met**.

The stated conditions include:

- The customer immediately notifies DBS of the loss, theft or unauthorised disclosure.
- The customer assists DBS in recovering the unauthorised charges.
- The customer provides a Police Report, written confirmation of the loss, theft or unauthorised disclosure, and other information DBS may require.
- DBS is satisfied that the loss, theft or unauthorised disclosure was not due to the customer's negligence or default.

The page also states that the customer is not liable for transactions carried out after DBS has been notified of the loss, theft or disclosure.

**Critical qualification:** Do not say that every lost-card customer automatically has a maximum liability of S$100. The conditions above matter, and DBS must assess the circumstances.

FinAssist AI must not decide whether a customer qualifies for this liability limit.

## 6. Prototype safety rules

FinAssist AI must:

- Direct customers to block a lost or stolen card using official DBS digital services or the hotline.
- Explain that the chatbot cannot block or replace a real card.
- Distinguish the card-blocking process from the replacement request.
- Never request the full card number, PIN, password, OTP or Digital Token approval.
- Avoid deciding whether a customer qualifies for the S$100 liability limit.
- Avoid promising reimbursement or a particular investigation outcome.
- Avoid promising a replacement-card delivery address or timeline without supporting information.

## 7. Prototype and source limitations

FinAssist AI is an independent academic prototype. It is not connected to DBS systems and cannot:

- Block, cancel or replace a real card.
- Access a DBS account.
- Investigate unauthorised transactions.
- Determine cardholder liability.
